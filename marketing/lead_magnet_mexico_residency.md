## 2. Checklist Content (Draft)

### Phase 1: Pre-Application (At the Consulate)
1.  **Financial Solvency Check:** Do you meet the current monthly income or savings requirements for Temporary or Permanent Residency? (Current 2024 figures included).
2.  **Passport Validity:** Ensure your passport has at least 6 months of validity and blank pages.
3.  **Application Form:** Download and print the official consulate form (one-sided only).
4.  **Passport Photos:** Specific dimensions required (Star-eye, white background, no glasses).
5.  **Proof of Income:** 6-12 months of bank statements or pay stubs, stamped or with QR code if required by your specific consulate.

### Phase 2: The Interview
6.  **Consulate Appointment:** How to use the "MiConsulado" booking system effectively.
7.  **Interview Prep:** Be ready to explain *why* you want to live in Mexico (avoid saying you want to work locally).
8.  **The Visa Sticker:** Verify the 180-day validity of your visa sticker once approved.

### Phase 3: The "Canje" (In Mexico)
9.  **FMM/Entry Port:** Ensure your entry is marked as "Canje" (30 days) not "Turismo" (180 days).
10. **INM Appointment:** Complete the process at the National Institute of Migration (INM) within 30 days of arrival to get your physical ID card.
