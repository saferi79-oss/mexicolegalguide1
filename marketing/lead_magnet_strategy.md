## 2. Lead Magnet Content (The PDF Resource)

### Step 1: Financial Solvency Audit
- Check if you meet the 2024 income/savings requirements for Temporary vs. Permanent Residency.
- **Tip:** Consulates look for *after-tax* monthly average or stable savings over the last 12 months.

### Step 2: Document Preparation
- Passport (valid for at least 6 months).
- Proof of legal stay in your current country (if not a citizen).
- 12 months of bank statements (stamped or QR-coded).

### Step 3: The Consulate Interview
- Booking via "MiConsulado."
- Common question: "Why do you want to live in Mexico?"
- **Crucial:** Never say you plan to look for local employment; residency is for those with foreign income.

### Step 4: The Visa Sticker
- Verify the 180-day validity.
- Ensure the Consulate officer returns your original bank statements.

### Step 5: Entering Mexico (The FMM)
- Enter via "Canje" status (30 days).
- **Caution:** If the immigration officer marks you as "Tourism" (180 days), you cannot finish your residency process.

### Step 6: The INM Appointment (Canje)
- Schedule your appointment within 30 days of arrival.
- Forms: Formato Básico and Solicitud de Trámite.

### Step 7: CURP & Physical Card
- Biometrics (fingerprints/photos).
- Receiving your plastic ID card.
