## 3. Upsell & Conversion Strategy

### Strategy 1: Post-Purchase Automation (The "Thank You" Upsell)
**Mechanism:** Gumroad Workflows.
- **Trigger:** Purchase of ANY individual guide.
- **Email (1 hour later):** "Glad you got the [Guide Name]! Since you're serious about your business in Mexico, upgrade to the [Related Bundle] for just an additional $XX."
- **Incentive:** Provide a unique discount code that makes the price difference between the single guide and the bundle attractive.

### Strategy 2: The "Migration" Lead Magnet (High Conversion)
**Mechanism:** Free Guide/Checklist.
- **Title:** "Mexico Residency & Visa Checklist: 2024 Edition"
- **Purpose:** Capture emails of foreigners (the primary target in the Business Plan).
- **Upsell:** The first email in the sequence should introduce the business guides if they are interested in working/investing in Mexico.

### Strategy 3: Tiered Pricing on Landing Page
**Layout:** Show individual guides at $27-$49, but highlight the **Bundles** as the "Best Value" options with a clear "Save $XX" badge.
