## 2. How to Configure in Gumroad (Owner's Guide)
1. Go to your **Gumroad Dashboard**.
2. Click on **Workflows** in the sidebar.
3. Click **New Workflow**.
4. **Name:** Give it a name (e.g., "Post-Purchase Follow-up").
5. **Trigger:** Select "After a product is purchased" and choose the specific product (or "All products").
6. **Schedule:**
   - For Email 1: Set to "0 hours" after purchase.
   - For Email 2: Set to "2 days" after purchase.
   - For Email 3: Set to "5 days" after purchase.
   - For Email 4: Set to "10 days" after purchase.
7. **Copy & Paste:** Use the subjects and bodies provided above.
8. **Test:** Send a test email to yourself to check formatting.
9. **Publish:** Toggle the workflow to "Active."
