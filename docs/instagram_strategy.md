# Instagram Automation & Content Strategy - @mexicolegalguide

## 1. Analysis of Current Presence
*   **Handle:** @mexicolegalguide
*   **Focus:** Educational legal tips for founders and foreigners in Mexico.
*   **Current Flow:** Discovery via content -> Bio Link (Carrd) -> Gumroad.
*   **Gap:** High friction between seeing a post and finding the specific product mentioned. No automated lead capture or direct-to-DM flows.

## 2. Recommended Automation Tools
*   **Metricool (Primary Scheduling & Analytics):**
    *   *Why:* Best-in-class analytics and easy scheduling for Reels, Carousels, and Stories. Allows "Link in Bio" customization that mimics a store.
    *   *Cost:* Free tier available; paid starts at ~$18/mo.
*   **ManyChat (DM Automation):**
    *   *Why:* Crucial for conversion. Convert comments ("Send me the link") into direct Gumroad links in DMs.
    *   *Flow:* Post about "IP in Mexico" -> User comments "IP" -> Bot sends link to the IP Guide on Gumroad.
    *   *Cost:* Free up to 500 contacts; Pro at $15/mo.

## 3. Automated Weekly Content Plan
This plan aims for 3 high-value posts per week + daily automated stories.

| Day | Format | Topic Pillar | Automation Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Reel | **Educational Tip** (e.g., "3 Mistakes when hiring in MX") | ManyChat: "HIRING" -> Link to Hiring Guide |
| **Tuesday** | Story | **Expertise/Persona** (Sabrina's background/daily life) | Automated repost of old top tip |
| **Wednesday** | Carousel | **Value/Checklist** (e.g., "Company Setup Checklist") | ManyChat: "SETUP" -> Link to Company Guide |
| **Thursday** | Story | **Social Proof** (Testimonial or Fiverr review) | Sticker: "Have a question?" -> Lead capture |
| **Friday** | Reel | **Direct Sales** (Deep dive into 1 specific guide) | ManyChat: "GUIDE" -> Store Link |
| **Saturday** | Story | **Weekend Q&A** (Reposting top questions) | Automated from previous Q&As |

## 4. Implementation Steps
1.  **Metricool Setup:** Connect Instagram account and schedule the first 2 weeks of content.
2.  **ManyChat Flows:** Create 3 specific keyword flows for the top-selling guides (Hiring, Company Setup, Startup Mistakes).
3.  **Direct-to-Store Links:** Ensure every Reel has a clear CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] to get the full guide."

## 5. KPIs to Track (Automated via Metricool)
*   **Reach/Impressions:** Measuring discovery.
*   **Engagement Rate:** Are the tips resonating?
*   **DM CTR:** Percentage of people who click the link sent by ManyChat.
*   **Follower Growth:** Brand authority building.
